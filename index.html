The big picture

Four stages worth understanding.

A nonprofit starts with a purpose and continues with responsible stewardship. Your state's requirements and the federal rules are different pieces of the plan.

1. Define the purpose

Decide who you will help, what you will do, and whether an existing organization could partner with you.

2. Build the organization

Choose your board and learn your state rules. Understand organizing documents, bylaws, and responsibilities.

3. Understand recognition

Learn whether and how to apply for federal tax-exempt status. Use current official forms and instructions.

4. Keep it accountable

Maintain good records, meet applicable reporting deadlines, and regularly review your programs and finances.

Questions, answered

A clear answer is a good beginning.

Is the consultation really free?
Yes. We are happy to explain the nonprofit startup steps and answer general questions at no charge. You do not have to buy a service or join our email list.
How do I request help?
Call Shai at 561-212-1192 for free consultation and advice, or use the consultation request form or optional Wizard helper. Someone from our team will get back to you within a few hours. Please include a phone number so we can arrange the conversation.
Can I do the work myself?
Yes. Our free guides, checklist, and official-source links are designed to help you understand and handle the process yourself. Government agencies may charge filing fees, even when you prepare the documents yourself.
What should I have ready for the conversation?
Your idea, the people or community you hope to serve, your state, and a few questions. You do not need completed forms or a finished business plan.
Can the Foundation do some of the work for me?
Yes. You can ask about optional document-preparation assistance. We explain its scope, any service charges, and government fees before you make a decision. Choosing paid help is never a condition of using our free educational resources.
Does nonprofit incorporation automatically give federal tax exemption?
No. State incorporation and federal tax-exempt recognition are separate. The right application and any exceptions depend on the organization. Our roadmap explains the distinction and links to the IRS.
Will I receive emails after asking for a consultation?
We use your request to respond about your questions. Weekly articles require a separate, voluntary email subscription. You can unsubscribe at any time.
Do you guarantee approval?
No. Only the relevant government agency can decide an application. We provide general education and explain the process; the site does not replace advice from your own lawyer or tax professional.
